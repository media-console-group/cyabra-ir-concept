Cyabra, Inc. · Nasdaq: CYAB

Cyabra uncovers disinformation spread online

Cyabra’s platform collects public data from social media and news sites and applies its own algorithms to assess authenticity and sentiment, so customers can identify fake accounts, harmful narratives and AI-generated text and images. It sells software subscriptions, on-premises licences and managed reports. The company’s filings also state substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A 10-Q Jun 30, Note 1

01The businessIn the filings’ own words

Software that looks for fake accounts and harmful narratives online

The 10-Q says Cyabra uses artificial intelligence and machine learning to monitor and analyze online conversations. It describes the targets of disinformation as corporations or public sector agencies, and says that in many cases they lack the tools or the expertise to find and counter it. 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A

  1. 1CollectThe platform gathers publicly available data from social media and news sites.
  2. 2AssessIts proprietary algorithms assess authenticity and sentiment.
  3. 3IdentifyThat lets it flag fake accounts, harmful narratives and GenAI text and images, and track the source and spread of information to measure its reach and impact.

Legacy Cyabra began offering its application to customers in 2022 and to date has generated limited revenues.

Quarterly report on Form 10-Q, MD&A 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A

How it earns revenue

SaaS subscription
Customers buy access to the company’s tools through a website. Subscriptions are non-refundable and generally run from several months to three years; customers usually pay in advance, which is recorded as deferred revenue and recognized ratably over the term. 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A
On-premises licence
Where a customer, particularly a governmental agency, wants to host the application on its own servers, the company sells a licence with a term of two to three years. Revenue is recognized ratably. 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A
Managed services
For customers who want a limited-purpose or trial use, the company prepares a report or a package of reports in-house on agreed criteria. Revenue is recognized when the deliverable is transferred. 10-Q Jun 30, MD&A

05DisclosuresWhat the company says about its own risks

Read these before the rest

The company’s own filings state substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. This is the passage, in its words, followed by the other items a reader of the filings would weigh.

Going concern, 10-Q Note 1 10-Q Jun 30, Note 1

Based on the projected cash flows and cash balance as of June 30, 2026, management is of the opinion that without further fund raising it will not have sufficient resources to enable it to continue its operating activities, including the development and marketing of its products for a period of 12 months from the balance sheet date of these consolidated financial statements. As a result, there is a substantial doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.

Management’s plans

Continuing commercialization of the Company’s products and securing sufficient funding through the sale of additional equity securities. There are no assurances however, that the Company will be successful in obtaining the level of financing needed for its operations.

If those plans do not succeed

If the Company is unsuccessful in commercializing its products and securing sufficient funding, it may need to reduce activities, curtail or even cease operations.
