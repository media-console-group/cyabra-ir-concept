# Cyabra, Inc. (Nasdaq: CYAB): Investor Relations concept

An independent design concept for an investor relations landing page. It is **not** Cyabra, Inc.'s official site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by Cyabra, Inc.

Figures come from the company's Forms 10-Q for the quarters ended March 31 and June 30, 2026, its Form 8-K of March 31, 2026 (the audited results of Legacy Cyabra for 2025 and 2024), its other Forms 8-K and its proxy statement through September 10, 2026, each linked beside the item. The 10-K filed March 26, 2026 is the pre-merger shell company's and is not used for operating results. The logo and the two illustrations are the company's own, from cyabra.com; the dilution plate is an original drawing from the share counts the filings state.
